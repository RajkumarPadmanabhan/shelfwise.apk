Scan to add

Point the camera at a book’s barcode and the title, author, description and cover fill in by themselves. You can type the ISBN instead.

Read your own PDFs & EPUBs

Attach a PDF or EPUB and tap Read. Pages curl over like paper, you can pinch or double-tap to zoom, and EPUBs re-flow at the text size you like. Bookmark pages, write notes, and pick up exactly where you stopped.

Goals, time & Rewind

Set a yearly reading goal and Shelfwise counts the time you actually spend reading. Rate books with stars. Rewind is a story-style recap of your month, year or all time (hours read, top-rated book, biggest month) that you can share or save.

Backgrounds, themes & fonts

Choose light, dark or follow your phone, then a background (Cream, Paper, Sage, Sky, Blush or Lavender) with an optional pattern: dots, ruled lines, grid paper or linen. Add an accent colour, a font (Nunito, Inter or Lora) and a text size.

100% local & private

Everything lives on your phone — your books, shelves, to-dos and stats. And when you want a safety copy, export a backup file you can share to Drive, email or a chat, then import it on your new phone.

How to install

Shelfwise isn't on the Play Store yet, so you install it straight from the APK. Three small steps:

  1. 1

    Download the APK

    On your Android phone, grab the latest shelfwise.apk from GitHub Releases.

    Download APK ⟶
  2. 2

    Allow the install

    Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow Install unknown apps for your browser or Files app, then tap Install.

    Play Protect may offer to scan the app first. That's normal for apps installed from outside the Play Store.

  3. 3

    Say hello

    Open Shelfwise and tell it your name. A quick tour shows you everything it can do. Then start lining up your books, and the owl will keep you company.

Updating to a new version

Shelfwise tells you when a new version is out and installs it for you (Settings → Updates). You can also download the new APK and install it over the old one. Your shelves, books and to-dos stay where they are.

Switching phones?

Before switching phones, use Settings → Share backup. Send the file to yourself, then use Settings → Import backup in Shelfwise on the new phone.

Frequently asked

Is it free?

Yes. Shelfwise is completely free — no ads, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases and no account to create.

Does it need internet?

No. Your shelves, reader, to-dos and stats all work offline. Shelfwise only goes online to check for a new version, to look up a book when you scan its barcode, or when you choose to send a backup through Drive, email or a chat app.

Where is my data?

On your phone, and only there. Your books, shelves, to-dos and stats are stored locally inside the app. There's no Shelfwise account and nothing is uploaded anywhere.

That also means uninstalling the app deletes your library — so share a backup to yourself now and then.

How do I move to a new phone?
  1. On your old phone, open Settings → Share backup and send the file somewhere you can reach it — Google Drive, email, or a chat with yourself. Turn on Include book files first if you want your PDFs and EPUBs to come along.
  2. Install Shelfwise on the new phone (see How to install).
  3. Open Settings → Import backup, pick the file, and your shelves come back just as you left them.
Why isn't it on the Play Store yet?

Shelfwise is a small, independent project, and publishing on Google Play takes a developer account, a testing period and a review. It may get there later.

Until then, GitHub Releases is the official home of the app — download the APK only from there or from this page.
